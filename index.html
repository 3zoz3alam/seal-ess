Tips

Mount the phone so it can't slide around. The app learns which way is forward during your first few speed changes (the "Forward" chip turns green).

Keep this screen open on the phone. CarPlay can show Maps at the same time. If you switch apps or lock the phone, iOS pauses the sound (web apps can't run in the background).

Your real pedal can't be read without modifying the car, so the app estimates it from how the car accelerates.
